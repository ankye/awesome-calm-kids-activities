# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this list.

## Good Fits

Good additions are usually:

- quiet art activities
- printable coloring pages
- breathing or grounding activities
- mindfulness ideas for kids
- gentle movement or kids yoga resources
- printable puzzles
- nature observation activities
- reading or journaling resources

## Not a Good Fit

Please avoid:

- medical claims or treatment advice
- pages that are mostly ads or popups
- paid-only bundles
- apps with no family, classroom, or kid-friendly use case
- unsafe, adult, gambling, or unrelated content
- duplicate links already listed in another section

## Format

Use one short line:

```md
- [Resource Name](https://example.com/) - Short, plain description.
```

Keep descriptions factual. No marketing language, keyword stuffing, or long pitches.

## Checks

Before opening a pull request, please check that:

- the link works
- the resource is kid-friendly
- the description fits the page being linked
- the page has at least some free content
